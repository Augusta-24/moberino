SUBTLEX-US word frequencies
===========================

data/subtlex-us-zipf.tsv is derived from the SUBTLEX-US frequency list published
by the Center for Reading Research, Ghent University. It keeps only three fields
(word, Zipf value, dominant part of speech) for lowercase alphabetic words of
3-8 letters. Rebuild it with tools/fetch_subtlex.py.

Source
------
https://www.ugent.be/pp/experimentele-psychologie/en/research/documents/subtlexus
http://crr.ugent.be/programs-data/subtitle-frequencies

Citation
--------
Brysbaert, M. & New, B. (2009). Moving beyond Kucera and Francis: A critical
evaluation of current word frequency norms and the introduction of a new and
improved word frequency measure for American English. Behavior Research
Methods, 41(4), 977-990.

van Heuven, W. J. B., Mandera, P., Keuleers, E. & Brysbaert, M. (2014).
SUBTLEX-UK: A new and improved word frequency database for British English.
Quarterly Journal of Experimental Psychology, 67(6), 1176-1190.
(introduces the Zipf scale used in this file)

Terms
-----
Ghent University offers the list for free download and it is widely reused in
published research, but the download page states no explicit licence or
redistribution terms. Confirm those terms with the Center for Reading Research
before publishing the derived file in a public repository. If you would rather
not redistribute it, delete data/subtlex-us-zipf.tsv and have contributors run
tools/fetch_subtlex.py; the generators fail with a clear message when the file
is absent, and nothing that ships to the browser depends on it.
